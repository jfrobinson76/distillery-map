## Speyside Distillery (Drumguish)
CONFIRMED CLOSED. Lease expired 2025; equipment removed and stored at Forsyths; Harvey's of Edinburgh declared it a "lost distillery" May 2025, planning a new site at Strathmashie.
Source: https://insidethecask.com/2025/05/02/speyside-distillery-becomes-scotlands-next-lost-distillery/
