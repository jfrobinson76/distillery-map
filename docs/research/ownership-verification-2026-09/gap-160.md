# Reconciling 154 (ours) vs 160 (CSI No.158, 8 Aug 2026) Scotch malt distilleries

Checked 22 Sep 2026. Read-only; nothing in the vault or any repo was edited.

## 1. What the arithmetic says

- CSI: 160 malt sites, 85 owners, 79% single-site. 0.79 x 85 = 67 single-site owners, so 18 multi-site owners hold 93 sites.
- Ours: 154 malt sites, 18 multi-site owners (matching CSI), so 61 single-site. 154 - 93 = 61.
- **The gap is exactly 6 single-site owners (61 -> 67).** Nothing may be added that makes an existing owner multi-site. That rules out Leven (Diageo), Portintruan (Elixir, already owns Tormore), Machrihanish (R&B, already owns Raasay) and any Glen Turner malt (La Martiniquaise).

## 2. SWA September 2026 list vs our CSV

SWA PDF (fetched 22 Sep 2026): https://www.scotch-whisky.org.uk/media/f2fm4u0u/list-of-current-operating-scotch-whisky-distilleries-for-public-website-september-2026.pdf

- 155 entries. 7 are grain (Cameronbridge, Girvan, Invergordon, Loch Lomond Grain, North British, Starlaw, Strathclyde). **148 malt.**
- We add 6 malt sites not on the SWA list: Glen Spey, Strathmill (Diageo), Abhainn Dearg, Eden Mill, Kythe, Stannergill. 148 + 6 = **154.** Our number reconciles to SWA exactly.
- CSI gets 6 more from outside SWA. SWA is not the source of CSI's extra sites.

## 6. Proposed definition and result

**Definition (recommended):** a Scotch malt distillery counts if it distilled malt whisky spirit on site in the 12 months to the count date, and is not publicly closed. Evidence can be the SWA operating list, or operator or trade-press confirmation of production. Sites in construction or commissioning, gin-only sites and closed sites are out.

**Result: 156 under a definition we can defend. That closes 2 of the 6.**

**Reaching CSI's 160 needs four "directory" inclusions.** All are single-site, so the owner arithmetic still works (61 + 6 = 67 singles, 85 owners, 79%):
1. Lone Wolf (BrewDog/Tilray). Distilled malt until its early-2026 closure.
2. Speyside (Harvey's). Closed May 2025. Owner is relocating.
3. Ben Cumhaill (Simpsons). Built but not yet distilling.
4. Saxa Vord (Shetland Distillery Co). Gin site with whisky ambitions.

You get 160 by taking the 156 and adding any four sites from this list or Kimbland. The Littler-style set (Speyside, Saxa Vord, Ben Cumhaill) plus Lone Wolf is the most likely match for CSI's method. We cannot prove that without CSI's list.

## 7. Uncertain

- Lerwick: we found no dated first-fill announcement. Operation is inferred from stills installed in 2024, opening in 2025, new make on sale and "single malt continues to mature".
- Isle of Skye Distillers may start distilling after July 2026. Recheck before any new count date.
- Glen Spey and Strathmill are missing from the SWA list. We count them as Diageo commissioned sites. If they are silent, both our count and CSI's would include idle plants.
- We could not see CSI's site list or its definition of "active".
