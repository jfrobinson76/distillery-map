# Originals — 2 Aug 2026 web-session artifacts

The first-pass research for the aging-inventory project, done on Claude.ai (web) and
downloaded on 2 Aug 2026. Kept as the audit trail back to source. **Superseded by
`docs/research/whiskey-aging-inventory-evidence.md`** — do not quote figures from
these files; several are known-wrong and corrected in the evidence doc:

- The CSV's US national figure (14.2m barrel equivalents) understated the proof-gallon
  conversion; the evidence doc derives ~25m barrels.
- The CSV found "no defensible figure" for Japan and India; both now have derived or
  floor figures (evidence doc Parts on Japan flow-to-stock and IMWA).
- `barrel_mound_map.png` is the original AI-generated mound map, rejected 3 Aug 2026
  for LinkedIn (crude mounds, illegible at feed size). The replacement is generated by
  `docs/social/build-map-card.mjs`.
- `china distiller map.webp` is the 2022 map referenced in the China entry's caveat
  (mixes operating, planned, unverified and Taiwanese sites).
- `Bestsellingwhiskies2024.jpeg` supports the India sales-vs-stock argument (8 Indian
  brands in the world top 20).

The CSV's value is its per-figure source URLs and pull-quotes (Distillery Trail, SWA,
Irish Examiner, EWG, Whisky Adventurer, Kavalan wiki) — the provenance chain for the
first-pass numbers.
