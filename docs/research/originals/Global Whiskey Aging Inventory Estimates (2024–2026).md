# Global Whiskey Aging Inventory Estimates (2024–2026)

*Methodology Note: There is no global census of maturing whiskey. The figures below represent the most credible recent disclosures from trade associations, governments, and industry reports. Units vary between physical casks, standardised barrel equivalents, and proof gallons. They are presented here to spark discussion on DistilleryMap.org, not as audited financial advice.*

## Primary Estimates

### 1. Scotland: ~22 Million Casks
* **Source:** Scotch Whisky Association (SWA), 2026 Facts & Figures.
* **Context:** “Some 22 million casks lie maturing in warehouses in Scotland waiting to be discovered - that is around 12bn 70cl bottles.” This is a physical cask count.

### 2. United States: ~17.1 Million Barrels (Kentucky) / ~14.2m Barrel Equivalents (National)
* **Source:** Kentucky Distillers’ Association (KDA) 2026 Economic Impact Report; Distilled Spirits Council of the U.S. (DISCUS) 2025 Mid-Year Report.
* **Context:** Kentucky reports a record 17.1 million barrels of spirits aging in 2024 (16.1m Bourbon). Nationally, DISCUS reports American Whiskey inventories reached nearly 1.5 billion proof gallons by end-2024. A proof gallon is a tax measure (one liquid gallon at 50% ABV). 1.5 billion proof gallons roughly equates to 14.2 million standard 53-gallon barrel equivalents at 50% ABV, suggesting the physical barrel count across all states and spirit types (including non-whiskey in Kentucky) is higher than the national whiskey-only volume equivalent.

### 3. Ireland: ~4.5 Million Casks
* **Source:** LYQD Irish Whiskey Supply Report 2026.
* **Context:** An industry estimate representing nearly nine years of global supply maturing in Irish bonded warehouses.

### 4. England: ~50,000 Casks
* **Source:** English Whisky Guild, 2023 forecast for end-2024.
* **Context:** An emerging market milestone representing over £1bn in maturing stock value.

### 5. Tasmania (Lark Distilling only): ~26,000 Barrel Equivalents
* **Source:** Lark Distilling Co. Ltd, ASX Half-Year Report (Dec 2025).
* **Context:** Lark alone holds 2.4 million litres at 43% ABV. While not a Tasmanian total, it highlights the scale of the island’s leading producer (roughly 26,000 pure-volume 200-litre equivalents).

### 6. Japan, Canada, and Rest of World: Data Gaps
* **Context:** Major producers like Suntory (Japan) and Hiram Walker (Canada) hold hundreds of thousands of casks individually, but no credible national aggregate is published. These regions represent significant “dark” inventory on the map.
